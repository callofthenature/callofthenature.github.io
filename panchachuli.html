Pithoragarh, Munsiyari & Almora

📍 Uttarakhand, India

An offbeat Kumaon Himalayan escape with close views of the Panchachuli range.

Kumaon Himalayan Escape

This Kumaon route runs from Pithoragarh to Munsiyari, facing the Panchachuli peaks, then to Almora.

How the Trip Works

Stay plan: Pithoragarh 1 night, Munsiyari 2 nights, Almora 1 night.

Quick Information

Duration4 Nights / 5 Days
RoutePithoragarh → Munsiyari → Almora
PickupKathgodam Railway Station
DropKathgodam Railway Station
Chat on WhatsApp

Detailed Itinerary

1
Arrive Pithoragarh
  • Arrive by afternoon, check into your hotel or homestay and relax.
  • Evening: Pithoragarh (Gorkha) Fort, Dev Singh Maidan, the local market and sunset over the Kumaon hills.
  • Kumaoni dinner at the hotel.
2
Pithoragarh to Munsiyari
  • After breakfast, drive towards Munsiyari (approx. 5–6 hours depending on road conditions and stops).
  • First glimpse of the Panchachuli range and sunset over the Kumaon Himalayas.
  • Short village walk, tea with mountain views and a bonfire, weather permitting.
3
Munsiyari Exploration
  • Morning: wake early for Panchachuli views. Visit Panchachuli View Point, Nanda Devi Temple, Munsiyari village and local viewpoints.
  • Afternoon, option A: trek to Khaliya Top for panoramic Himalayan views.
  • Afternoon, option B: an easy day with a village walk, Tribal Heritage Museum, Darkot village, handicraft shopping and scenic cafés.
4
Munsiyari to Almora
  • Start early after breakfast. This is a long transfer of approx. 7–8 hours, depending on route, road conditions and stops.
  • Evening in Almora: Mall Road, the old market, local shops and traditional Kumaoni sweets.
  • Sunset from a suitable viewpoint.
5
Almora Sightseeing & Departure
  • Morning: Kasar Devi Temple, Bright End Corner, Chitai Golu Devta Temple and Almora market.
  • Drop at Kathgodam Railway Station.
